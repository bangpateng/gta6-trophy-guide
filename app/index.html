Read this first: the one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude asks you to meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida — and one of them can pass you by. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason’s house in the morning, talk to her in the kitchen when she mentions meditating, follow her to the balcony and accept. That moment is gone once Chapter 1 ends. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 save slots) and one more before the final mission. Whether the balcony meditation is strictly required for the trophy is still unconfirmed — do it anyway.

Sources: KeenGamer platinum roadmap; PlanetGTA6 trophy list.

Roadmap to the Platinum

  1. Phase 1 — Story first 60–80 hours

    Play all five chapters and finish the final mission for the Gold You Gotta Know When to Get Off. Along the way: do Lucia’s balcony meditation before leaving Chapter 1, scan unfamiliar vehicles with the WAiNK app, study animals with binoculars, and take the optional missions that introduce fishing, hunting and robberies. Keep manual saves — there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus.

  2. Phase 2 — 100% completion ~100 hours

    Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the checklist for A Long Road, the hardest trophy. You do not need everything in the game — only the numbers in the table below. Stop each category at its number.

  3. Phase 3 — Cleanup 10–20 hours

    Finish the stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and three more for Strangers & Freaks), the remaining meditations, and the miscellaneous trophies. Then check the tracker below — the Platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops with the last one.

  • Cheats disable trophies — and you cannot save while one is active. Play the whole Platinum run clean.
  • No online trophies. All 55 are earnable solo, in one playthrough, with free roam after the story.
  • Same 55 on Xbox as achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore; the Platinum itself is worth 0 G there.

How accurate is this guide?

The trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official list published on PlayStation Network on 2026-11-18, cross-checked by PlanetGTA6 against Exophase, PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements. Every guide below is written in this guide’s own words and names its sources — a trophy with no source gets no credit, so none is left unsourced.

  • 46 confirmed — the requirement is in the official description and corroborated by the published guides.
  • 7 partial — the requirement is clear, but the exact spot or method is still being confirmed (Fair Game; In The Beginning…; Capital Offense; Let’s Go Bowling; Personal Best; Actions Speak Louder; Ten Pin).
  • 2 unconfirmed — no published guide has verified how to unlock them yet, and this guide says so instead of inventing a method (Bad Luck, I Guess; The Truth Is Out There).

The game only just came out, so the two unconfirmed trophies are flagged honestly in their cards. Each card carries its own status badge.
